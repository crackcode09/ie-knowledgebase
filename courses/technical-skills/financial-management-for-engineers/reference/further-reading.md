# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions, authorship details and availability were **not verified** with the publishers when this list was written, so check the current edition first.

- Brealey, R. A., Myers, S. C., and Allen, F. *Principles of Corporate Finance*. McGraw-Hill. The standard corporate-finance text: capital budgeting, cost of capital, leasing, mergers.
- Newnan, D. G., Eschenbach, T. G., and Lavelle, J. P. *Engineering Economic Analysis*. Oxford University Press. Time value of money, depreciation and project comparison, written for engineers.
- Horngren, C. T., et al. *Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis*. Pearson. Budgets, flexible budgets, variance analysis and responsibility accounting.
- Berman, K., and Knight, J. *Financial Intelligence*. Harvard Business Review Press. A plain-language guide to reading financial statements, aimed at non-financial managers.
- Koller, T., Goedhart, M., and Wessels, D. *Valuation: Measuring and Managing the Value of Companies*. Wiley. DCF and multiples in depth.

**Primary sources for rules that change.** Use your country's tax authority for depreciation schedules and rates (in the US, the IRS publishes depreciation guidance). Use the relevant accounting-standards body for lease and revenue rules (FASB for US GAAP, IASB for IFRS). Neither was consulted for this course.
