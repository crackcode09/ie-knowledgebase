# Further Reading

The titles below are listed from the course author's general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** against the publishers when this list was written, so check the current edition before you buy.

- Montgomery, D. C. *Design and Analysis of Experiments*. Wiley. The standard engineering text, covering every topic in this course.
- Box, G. E. P., Hunter, J. S., and Hunter, W. G. *Statistics for Experimenters*. Wiley. Strong on 2^k factorials, fractions and the sequential approach.
- Montgomery, D. C., and Runger, G. C. *Applied Statistics and Probability for Engineers*. Wiley. Its chapters on ANOVA and factorials are a gentler entry point.
- Myers, R. H., Montgomery, D. C., and Anderson-Cook, C. M. *Response Surface Methodology*. Wiley. The next step after Module 07's curvature test.
- Lenth, R. V. (1989). "Quick and easy analysis of unreplicated factorials." *Technometrics*, 31(4). The source of the PSE method.
- NIST/SEMATECH *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*, chapter 5 (Process Improvement). Free online.

Software: the Python packages used here (statsmodels, scipy) are documented at their project sites. R users can try the `FrF2` and `DoE.base` packages for fractional designs.
