## Facilitator notes

- **Session plan (2 hours each).** About 40 minutes of concept, 50 minutes of hands-on work on the learner's own material, and 30 minutes of sharing and feedback. Ask learners to bring a real presentation or report they gave recently. Every session improves that artifact.
- **Session 01:** show the draft deck *before* the error list and ask the group to find problems. Learners usually spot text density first and the buried ask last.
- **Session 02:** pairs interview each other as "the audience" for 10 minutes, then write one another's purpose statement.
- **Session 03:** the action-title exercise is the single highest-value activity. Have learners read only their titles aloud.
- **Session 04:** run a Q&A drill. One learner presents for 2 minutes and three others ask prepared tough questions. Debrief on brevity and honesty.
- **Session 05:** give everyone the raw `05_scrap_before_cleanup.png` and 15 minutes to redesign it (any tool), then compare.
- **Session 06:** each table builds a fishbone for "our meetings don't reach decisions" for their own workplace.
- **Sessions 07–08:** use the same rubric for every final presentation so scores are comparable. Collect the comments and do a Pareto as a group.
- **Readability scores:** the syllable counter is a heuristic, and scores can differ by a grade or so from other tools. Use them to compare drafts, not as absolute grades.
