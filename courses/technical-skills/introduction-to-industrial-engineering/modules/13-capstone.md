# Module 13 — Capstone and Skill Check

**Time:** 2–3 h

## Part A — Skill check

1. Give a one-sentence definition of industrial engineering.
2. Which pioneers are associated with (a) time study, (b) motion study and therbligs?
3. Name the five ASME process chart symbols.
4. Apply ECRS to "walk to printer to collect a label for each box."
5. Observed 30 s, rating 110%, allowance 15%. Standard time?
6. Element s = 2.0 s, mean 20 s. Cycles needed for ±5% at 95%?
7. Work sampling: 600 observations, 90 idle. Idle share and 95% margin?
8. 80% learning curve, first unit 10 h. Time for unit 8?
9. NIOSH: H = 40 cm, all other multipliers 1.0. RWL? Lifting index for 15 kg?
10. Job points 300; wage line pay = 4.23 + 0.0626 × points. Line pay?
11. Standard 1.2 min/unit; worker produces 450 units in 8 h. Efficiency and standard-hour-plan pay at $20/h?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. IE designs, improves and installs integrated systems of people, materials, information, equipment and energy.
2. (a) Frederick Taylor; (b) Frank and Lillian Gilbreth.
3. Operation ○, transport ⇨, inspection □, delay D, storage ▽.
4. Eliminate (print labels in batches at the bench or pre-printed with the pick list), or rearrange (printer at the bench).
5. 30 × 1.10 × 1.15 = 37.95 s.
6. (1.96 × 2.0 / (0.05 × 20))² = 3.92² = 15.4 → 16.
7. 15%; ±1.96 × √(0.15 × 0.85 / 600) = ±2.9 points.
8. 10 × 0.8³ = 5.12 h.
9. HM = 25/40 = 0.625 → RWL = 23 × 0.625 = 14.4 kg; LI = 15/14.4 = 1.04.
10. 4.23 + 0.0626 × 300 = $23.01.
11. Standard hours = 450 × 1.2 / 60 = 9.0 h → 112.5%; pay = 9.0 × $20 = $180.
</details>

## Part B — Capstone project

Pick a task you can observe (at work, or a home/office task such as packing an online order):

1. **Define** the process: SIPOC-level start and end, steps (process chart).
2. **Methods:** apply ECRS and motion-economy principles; propose a better method.
3. **Measure:** time study of the improved method — elements, ≥ 10 cycles, rating, allowances, standard time, cycles-needed check.
4. **Work sampling:** 50+ random observations of how time is spent (yourself or a team).
5. **Workplace:** check work height and reach against anthropometric guidance; evaluate any lift with NIOSH.
6. **Layout:** from–to chart and load–distance for the area; propose a better arrangement.
7. **People:** a short job description, a 4-factor point evaluation, and a skills matrix.
8. **Improvement:** one root cause analysis (5-Why) and one Lean change; show before/after data.
9. **Staffing:** operators needed from your standard and a demand assumption.

Templates: `templates/ie-worksheets.md`. Score each item 0 (missing) / 1 (done) / 2 (done with data and clear reasoning). 14+ of 18: ready to apply IE methods on the job.
