# Module 3 — Motion Study

**Time:** 75 min

## Learning objectives

1. Describe hand motions with therbligs.
2. Apply the principles of motion economy.
3. Use a two-hand chart to improve a workstation.

## 1. Therbligs

Frank and Lillian Gilbreth broke manual work into basic elements, named **therbligs** ("Gilbreth" spelled backward, roughly). Commonly listed:

| Effective (needed) | Ineffective (targets to remove) |
|---|---|
| Reach, Move, Grasp, Release, Use, Assemble, Disassemble, Pre-position | Search, Select, Hold, Unavoidable delay, Avoidable delay, Plan, Rest, Position, Inspect* |

*Inspect can be necessary but is often reduced by design.

## 2. Principles of motion economy

Grouped as in Ralph Barnes's classic *Motion and Time Study*:

**Use of the human body**
- Both hands should begin and end motions at the same time and not be idle at the same time (except rest).
- Arms move in opposite, symmetrical directions.
- Use the lowest body-member classification that does the job (fingers before wrist before arm before shoulder).
- Use momentum; prefer smooth curved motions to sudden direction changes.

**Arrangement of the workplace**
- Fixed places for all tools and materials.
- Tools and materials in front of and close to the worker, within the normal working area.
- Gravity-feed bins and drop delivery.
- Good lighting; chair and work surface height adjustable (Module 6).

**Design of tools and equipment**
- Use fixtures and jigs instead of hands to hold work.
- Combine tools; pre-position tools.
- Design handles for the hand.

## 3. Two-hand chart — case (bagging at the present bench)

| Left hand | Right hand | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Hold bag open | Reach to part pile | Left hand idle-holding |
| Hold bag open | Search and select part | Search |
| Hold bag open | Move part to bag, release | — |
| (repeat 8×) | (repeat 8×) | One-handed work |
| Release bag | Reach for sealer | — |

**Improved:** bag held open on a fixture (stand); parts pre-positioned in a kit tray; **both hands** pick symmetrical pairs and drop into the bag. Hold and search disappear; the inspect-and-bag element falls from ~14 s to a target of ~9 s (to be verified by time study).

## Check yourself

A packer holds a part with the left hand while the right hand labels it. Which principle and which fix?
