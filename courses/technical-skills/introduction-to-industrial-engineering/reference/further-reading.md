# Further Reading

| Source | Why read it |
|---|---|
| Freivalds, A., & Niebel, B. W. *Niebel's Methods, Standards, and Work Design*, 13th ed. McGraw-Hill, 2013. | The standard text on methods, time study, work sampling and wage payment |
| Barnes, R. M. *Motion and Time Study: Design and Measurement of Work*, 7th ed. Wiley, 1980. | Classic text; principles of motion economy |
| Kanawaty, G. (ed.). *Introduction to Work Study*, 4th ed. International Labour Office, 1992. | Method study, work measurement, allowances (freely available from the ILO) |
| Taylor, F. W. *The Principles of Scientific Management*. Harper, 1911. | Primary source on scientific management (public domain) |
| Gilbreth, F. B., & Gilbreth, L. M. *Applied Motion Study*. 1917. | Motion study (public domain) |
| Salvendy, G. (ed.). *Handbook of Industrial Engineering*, 3rd ed. Wiley, 2001. | Broad reference across IE |
| Waters, T. R., Putz-Anderson, V., Garg, A., & Fine, L. J. (1993). "Revised NIOSH equation for the design and evaluation of manual lifting tasks." *Ergonomics*, 36(7), 749–776. | NIOSH lifting equation |
| NIOSH. *Applications Manual for the Revised NIOSH Lifting Equation*. Publication 94-110, 1994. | Multiplier tables and procedure |
| Kroemer, K. H. E., & Grandjean, E. *Fitting the Task to the Human*, 5th ed. Taylor & Francis, 1997. | Ergonomics and workplace design |
| Tompkins, J. A., et al. *Facilities Planning*, 4th ed. Wiley, 2010. | Layout and material handling |
| Muther, R. *Systematic Layout Planning*. 1961. | SLP method |
| Wright, T. P. (1936). "Factors affecting the cost of airplanes." *Journal of the Aeronautical Sciences*, 3(4), 122–128. | Learning curve |
| Herzberg, F., Mausner, B., & Snyderman, B. *The Motivation to Work*. Wiley, 1959. | Two-factor theory |
| Hackman, J. R., & Oldham, G. R. (1976). "Motivation through the design of work." *Organizational Behavior and Human Performance*, 16(2), 250–279. | Job characteristics model |
