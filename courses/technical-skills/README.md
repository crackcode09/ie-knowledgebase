# Technical Skills

Core IE technical topics: statistics, design of experiments, process modeling, reliability, financial management.

## Courses

| Course | Topic | Length | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Introduction to Industrial Engineering](introduction-to-industrial-engineering/README.md) | What IEs do; methods and motion study; time study, work sampling and standards; ergonomics; layout; job analysis, evaluation and pay; Lean, Six Sigma, RCA; basic statistics | ~3 days | draft |
| [Applied Statistics](applied-statistics/README.md) | Data collection systems; descriptive statistics; hypergeometric, binomial, Poisson, normal, exponential, t, chi-square, F; CIs and tests; non-normal data; correlation; linear and multiple regression | ~2 days | draft |
| [Design of Experiments](design-of-experiments/README.md) | Completely randomized, randomized block and Latin square designs; two-factor ANOVA and interaction; balanced vs unbalanced; replication and power; 2^k full and fractional factorials; operating levels with cost | ~2 days | draft |
| [Financial Management for Engineers](financial-management-for-engineers/README.md) | Business organization; balance sheet, income statement, cash flow; ratios and dashboards; budgets and variances; time value of money; sources and cost of capital; ROI, NPV, IRR; lease vs buy; sensitivity and Monte Carlo; break-even; valuation and M&A | ~2 days | draft |
| [Technical Communications](technical-communications/README.md) | Presenting quantitative work to managers: common errors, audience analysis, purpose and one-sentence message, answer-first structure, listening and Q&A, data visualization, process-improvement tools for presentations, quality presentations, capstone | 9 × 2 h | draft |
| [Reliability Engineering Excellence](reliability-engineering-excellence/README.md) | Reliability role and program; risk and criticality; design for reliability, RBD, reliability growth; MTBF/MTTR/availability; RCM and TPM; Pareto, exponential, normal and Weibull (with suspensions); LCC and optimum reliability cost; RCA, FRACAS, FMECA; PM optimization; five PdM technologies | ~5 days | draft |
| [Process Modeling and Simulation](process-modeling-and-simulation/README.md) | Discrete-event process models: build to think, validation, design exploration, resilience testing, operating procedures, model-based FMEA, business case, teaching Lean | ~7 h | draft |
