# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions before buying.

- Freivalds, A., and Niebel, B. W. *Niebels' Methods, Standards, and Work Design*. McGraw-Hill. Includes chapters on standard data and predetermined time systems.
- Zandin, K. B. (ed.). *Maynard's Industrial Engineering Handbook*. McGraw-Hill. Includes work measurement, standard data and PMTS chapters.
- Kanawaty, G. (ed.). *Introduction to Work Study*. International Labour Office (ILO).
- Montgomery, D. C., Peck, E. A., and Vining, G. G. *Introduction to Linear Regression Analysis*. Wiley. Covers diagnostics, multicollinearity and validation in depth.
- Kutner, M. H., Nachtsheim, C. J., and Neter, J. *Applied Linear Regression Models*. McGraw-Hill.
- The MTM and MOST systems are taught through their licensing bodies and training providers. Check them for current system details.
- Microsoft's Excel documentation for `LINEST` and the Analysis ToolPak. Menu paths change between versions.
