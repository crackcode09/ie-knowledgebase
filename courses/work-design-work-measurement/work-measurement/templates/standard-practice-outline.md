# Work Measurement Standard Practice (Outline)

1. Purpose and scope
2. Definitions: qualified worker, normal pace (benchmarks), standard time, on/off-standard time
3. Roles: who sets, approves, maintains and audits standards
4. Methods: selection of technique by work type; minimum cycles / accuracy targets
5. Rating: system used, training and calibration frequency, acceptance limits (e.g. ±5 points)
6. Allowances: categories, values, convention, source, review frequency
7. Documentation: study file contents and retention
8. Communication: informing employees; issuing and explaining standards
9. Maintenance: re-study triggers; periodic audit cycle; tracking standard age
10. Labor reporting: definitions of efficiency, utilization, productivity; off-standard reason codes
11. Review and grievance procedure
12. Ethics
