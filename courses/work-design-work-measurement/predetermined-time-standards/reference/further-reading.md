# Sources and Further Reading

## Opened and used for the MODAPTS facts in this course

- Eisbrenner PG, "About MODAPTS®": https://eisbrennerpg.com/about-modapts/ (acronym; 1 MOD = 0.129 s; developed by G. Chris Heyde, introduced 1966; copyright assigned to the IMA in 1990; letter-plus-integer codes; no stopwatch or rating; claimed ±5 % accuracy).
- 6sigma.us, "MODAPTS: Modular Arrangement of Predetermined Time Standards": https://www.6sigma.us/work-measurement/modapts-modular-arrangement-of-predetermined-time-standards/ (acronym; MOD multiples of 0.129 s; body-part classification).
- Justia Trademarks, International MODAPTS Association, Inc.: https://trademark.justia.com/owners/international-modapts-association-inc-399044 (trademark ownership).
- Wikipedia, predetermined motion time system article (reached via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MODAPTS): MTM released in 1948; TMU = 0.036 s; ILO's count of about 200 systems (Kanawaty 1992); detailed vs higher-level systems.

These are practitioner and secondary sources. Where they disagree (for example on how "MOD" is expanded), the IMA's official material is authoritative.
