# Module 06 — History of MODAPTS

## Objectives

- Place MODAPTS in the history of motion and time study.
- State the key public facts about its origin and ownership.

## A short timeline

| When | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Early 1900s | Frank and Lillian Gilbreth break manual work into elemental motions ("therbligs") | General knowledge; **not verified here** |
| 1948 | MTM (Methods-Time Measurement) released, the first widely used PMTS | [Wikipedia: PMTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MODAPTS) |
| 1950s | G. Chris Heyde learns MTM-1 and MTM-2 and looks for a simpler system with whole-number values | [Eisbrenner PG](https://eisbrennerpg.com/about-modapts/) |
| 1966 | MODAPTS introduced by Heyde (Heyde Dynamics Pty. Ltd., Australia) | [Eisbrenner PG](https://eisbrennerpg.com/about-modapts/) |
| 1990 | Copyright assigned to the International MODAPTS Association | [Eisbrenner PG](https://eisbrennerpg.com/about-modapts/) |
| 1992 | The ILO reports about 200 predetermined time systems in existence | Kanawaty (1992), cited on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MODAPTS) |
| Today | IMA offers practitioner certification; the name is a registered trademark of the IMA | [Trademark record](https://trademark.justia.com/owners/international-modapts-association-inc-399044) |

## Why the history matters

MODAPTS was a deliberate **simplification** of earlier, detailed systems:

- whole-number values
- a small set of codes
- classification by **body member** rather than by measured distance

The trade-off is the same for every PMTS: a simpler system is faster to learn and apply, but its time for an individual motion is less exact. On longer tasks, errors on individual motions tend to average out.
