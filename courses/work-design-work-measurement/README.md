# Work Design & Work Measurement

Work measurement, standard data, predetermined time systems, facilities and workplace design.

## Courses

| Course | Topic | Length | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Work Measurement](work-measurement/README.md) | Need for and impact of standards; standard practice; documentation and charting; methods analysis and work design; defining work; time study, elemental analysis, rating, allowances, computations; labor reporting; job analysis; work sampling; standard data and PMTS; learning curves and staffing | ~2 days | draft |
| [Standard Data Systems](standard-data-systems/README.md) | Microscopic vs macroscopic data; advantages and disadvantages; element structure and data sheets; correlation and regression (Excel LINEST/ToolPak); developing models with dummies, transformations and VIF; evaluating with residuals, intervals and hold-out validation; quoting, capacity and maintenance | ~2 days | draft |
| [Staffing Models & Human Capital Management](staffing-models/README.md) | Staffing models in the HCM framework; terminology; seven data types; work measurement for staffing; available hours and relief factors; workload, coverage, queueing (Erlang C), ratio and benchmark models; forecasting; supply, attrition and hiring plans; development phases, back-testing and sensitivity; budgets and executive summaries | ~2 days | draft |
| [Predetermined Time Standards (MODAPTS Topics)](predetermined-time-standards/README.md) | Follows the MODAPTS practitioner outline item by item: PMTS basics, acronym, code symbol, history, modular concept, movement classes and rules, get vs put, conscious control, GET/PUT elements, auxiliary activities, practice analyses with an original teaching code card (not the official card), method and ergonomic improvement | ~3 days | draft |
| [Facilities and Workplace Design](facilities-and-workplace-design/README.md) | Facilities strategy and levels of design; layout planning and project plan; P-Q, inventory, usage, flow and processes; planning units, affinities, space constraints, configuration diagrams; material flow and layout exchange; cellular and workstation layouts; process charting, time estimates, cell capacity, people and machines; ergonomics, motion economy; infrastructure, scheduling, containers, balancing, supervision, rewards; cell configurations and layout procedures | ~2 days | draft |
| [Detailed Motion Time Analysis (MTM-1 Topics)](detailed-motion-time-analysis/README.md) | Methods analysis and two-handed charts; reach, move, turn, apply pressure, grasp, position, release, disengage, eye and body motions; simultaneous motions; writing analyses; SIMO-based improvement; developing standards; 11 labs. Original teaching data card (not MTM data) | ~10 days with labs | draft |
