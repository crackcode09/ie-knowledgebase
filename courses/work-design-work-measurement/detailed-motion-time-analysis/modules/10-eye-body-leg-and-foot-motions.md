# Module 10 — Eye, Body, Leg and Foot Motions

## Objectives

- Code eye travel and focus, and body, leg and foot motions.
- Know when eye time is limiting and when it is hidden in hand time.

## Eye motions

| Code | cs | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ET<cm> | 2 + 0.3 × cm | Eye travel between points at normal reading distance |
| EF | 20 | Eye focus: recognize a detail (a mark, a defect, a reading) |

Code eye time **only when it limits the work**: inspection, reading, checking. During ordinary reaches and moves the eyes move at the same time and are hidden.

## Body, leg and foot motions

| Code | cs | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| FM | 25 | Foot motion (pedal) |
| LM | 30 | Leg motion |
| SS | 50 | Sidestep |
| TBD | 55 | Turn body |
| WK<n> | 45 per step | Walk |
| BD / ABD | 90 / 95 | Bend / arise from bend |
| SIT / STD | 120 / 140 | Sit / stand from sitting |

Body motions often dominate an analysis. In Lab 10 (getting a box from the floor 3 steps away), walking, bending and arising are **455 of 521 cs (87 %)**.
