# Module 7 — Day 2: Root Causes and Solution Ideas

**Time:** 2.5 h

## Learning objectives

1. Run Day 2 of an event: from current-state findings to root causes and a list of solution ideas.
2. Use a fishbone, 5-Why and scatter plot inside a kaizen.
3. Facilitate brainstorming that produces many practical ideas.

The tools are covered in depth in *Root Cause Analysis & Problem Solving* (Modules 5, 6 and 9); this module shows how to use them at kaizen speed.

## 1. Day 2 schedule

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Review Day 1 findings; agree the 3–4 problems to attack (constraint, inventory, motion, changeover) |
| 08:30 | Fishbone per problem; 5-Why on the top causes |

## 2. Case — why is preload the constraint?

**Fishbone highlights:** long changeover (method), fixture loading inside the cycle (method), downtime from shim adjustments (machine), parts not always at the station (material), first-part check by quality tech who isn't always available (people).

**5-Why on downtime:**

| Why? | Answer |
|---|---|
| Why is preload uptime only 85%? | 50-minute changeovers and stops to adjust shims |
| Why do changeovers take 50 min? | Fixture and shims are fetched after the machine stops; fixture has 8 bolts; trial-and-error preload setting |
| Why trial-and-error? | No preset gauge; setting depends on the setter's experience |
| Why no preset gauge? | Changeover was never studied; sizes were added one at a time over years |

**Quick data check:** time 6–12 changeovers and plot changeover time against the number of trial parts. If the points rise together, trial-and-error setting is driving changeover time. The element-by-element SMED study (Module 9) confirms it is the largest single element (15 of 50 minutes).

## 3. Brainstorming

- State the problem as a question: "How might we cut preload changeover to under 20 minutes?"
- **Brainwriting first:** 5 minutes silent, one idea per sticky note — avoids the loudest-voice effect.
- Then share, build on others, no criticism, quantity over quality, wild ideas welcome.
- Cluster and select: do-now (no approval needed), try this week, needs more work.

**Output for the case (selection):** pre-stage fixture and shims (do-now); quick clamps + locating pins (try this week — maintenance makes them Wed); preset preload gauge (try); move fixture loading off the constraint (do-now); point-of-use parts (do-now); U-cell layout (try — Days 3–4).

## Check yourself

Why do quick data checks on Day 2 rather than trusting the team's experience?
