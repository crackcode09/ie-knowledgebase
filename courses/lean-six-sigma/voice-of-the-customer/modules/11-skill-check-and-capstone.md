# Module 11 — Skill Check and Capstone

**Time:** 45 min + capstone

## Part A — Quiz

1. Name three different customers of a company's invoicing process.
2. "Easy to order" — output or service requirement?
3. Functional answer "I like it," dysfunctional answer "I dislike it." Kano category?
4. A feature: A = 20, O = 10, M = 60, I = 10. Compute better and worse. What kind of need is it?
5. 400 respondents, 35% satisfied. 95% MOE?
6. How many responses for ±4 points at 95% (p unknown)?
7. What's wrong with: "Do you agree our fast, friendly service meets your needs?"
8. Customers' acceptable response times: 10th percentile = 4 h, median = 8 h. Which value would you use as the upper spec limit, and why?
9. In a C&E matrix, an input scores 9 on one output (weight 10) and 0 on everything else. Another scores 3 on four outputs (weights 10, 8, 6, 4). Totals? What else should you consider?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. E.g., the customer's accounts-payable clerk, the customer's purchasing manager, our finance/collections team (internal), auditors.
2. Service requirement.
3. One-dimensional (O).
4. Better = (20 + 10)/100 = 0.30; worse = −(10 + 60)/100 = −0.70 → must-be.
5. 1.96 × √(0.35 × 0.65 / 400) = ±4.7 points.
6. 1.96² × 0.25 / 0.04² = 600.25 → 601.
7. Leading and double-barreled ("fast, friendly"); agree/disagree format encourages acquiescence.
8. About 4 h, which satisfies ~90% of customers; the median would leave half dissatisfied.
9. 90 vs 3 × 28 = 84. The first is the only strong driver of a high-weight CTQ — it may matter more than its total suggests.
</details>

## Part B — Capstone

For a product or process you work with:

1. Identify customers (paying, using, influencing, internal) and segments.
2. Collect at least 15 verbatims from interviews/observation and existing data; build an affinity diagram.
3. Classify the needs as output vs service and, for 3 candidate features, design Kano questions.
4. Draft a 10-question survey (importance + satisfaction for the top needs, one Kano pair, one acceptable-time question); pilot it with 3 people.
5. State the sample size needed for ±5 points overall and the expected margin of error per segment.
6. Build a CTQ tree with operational definitions for at least 3 CTQs.
7. Turn one CTQ into a specification using customer data (or explain what data you'd need).
8. Build a C&E matrix linking the CTQs to at least 6 process inputs; pick the top 3 to investigate.

Templates: `templates/voc-templates.md`. Score each item 0 (missing) / 1 (present) / 2 (traceable to customer data and checked). 13+ of 16: ready to lead VOC work on a project.
