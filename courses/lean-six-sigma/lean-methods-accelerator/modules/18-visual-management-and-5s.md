# Module 18 — Visual Management and 5S

**Time:** 50 min

## Learning objectives

1. Implement 5S and audit it.
2. Design visual management that shows normal vs abnormal at a glance.

## 1. 5S

| S | Meaning | Manifold cell |
|---|---|---|
| **Sort** | Remove what isn't needed | Red-tag obsolete fixtures, extra carts, broken tools |
| **Set in order** | A place for everything, at point of use, labeled | Shadow boards for each CNC's tools; fixture racks by family |
| **Shine** | Clean and inspect | Daily 10-min clean of machine and chip conveyors — spot leaks and wear |
| **Standardize** | Make the first three routine | Photos of the standard condition; checklists |
| **Sustain** | Keep it going | Weekly audits; leaders check during gemba walks |

**Audit** each S on a 0–5 scale weekly; trend the score. Sustain is the hardest S — it depends on leaders, not posters.

5S is not housekeeping for its own sake: it shortens changeovers (Module 15), makes abnormalities visible, and is the base of TPM's autonomous maintenance (Module 19).

## 2. Visual management

The test: can anyone walking by tell in **30 seconds** whether the area is on plan, and if not, why?

| Tool | Shows |
|---|---|
| Hour-by-hour board | Target (15/hour at takt 4.0) vs actual, reason for misses |
| Andon | Help needed; response time |
| Floor markings | Locations, walkways, WIP limits (empty = starved, full = stop upstream) |
| Kanban and heijunka box | What to make next |
| Shadow boards, labels | Where things belong; what's missing |
| Machine status lights | Running / stopped / changeover |
| Team board (KPIs, actions) | Trends and who is fixing what |

Visual controls should prompt **action**, not decoration: each display needs an owner and a response rule.

## Check yourself

An area scores 5 on Sort and Set in order in month 1 and 2 in month 4. What's missing?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Standardize and Sustain: routines, audits and leader attention that keep the condition.
</details>
