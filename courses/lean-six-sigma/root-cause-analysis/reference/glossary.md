# Glossary

**5W2H** — What, Where, When, Who, Which, How much, How (detected): questions to describe a problem.

**5-Why, 1-How** — Repeatedly ask why to reach a root cause, then one how to define the countermeasure.

**8D** — Eight-discipline problem-solving format.

**Containment** — Immediate action to protect the customer; doesn't remove the cause.

**Correction** — Fixing the nonconforming product.

**Corrective action** — Removing the root cause to prevent recurrence.

**Corrective action matrix** — Tracking table of actions, owners, dates and verification.

**Correlation coefficient (r)** — Strength and direction of a linear relationship, −1 to +1.

**Decision matrix** — Weighted scoring of solutions against agreed criteria.

**Fishbone (cause-and-effect) diagram** — Organizes potential causes into categories.

**Is / Is-Not** — Compares where a problem occurs with where it could but doesn't.

**Logistic regression** — Models the probability of a binary outcome.

**Multicollinearity** — Correlation among inputs that makes regression coefficients unstable.

**Multiple regression** — Models a continuous Y from several Xs.

**Odds ratio** — Multiplicative change in odds for a change in an input.

**Preventive action** — Applying the lesson to stop the problem elsewhere.

**R² / adjusted R²** — Share of variation explained / penalized for number of terms.

**Root cause** — The cause that, when removed, prevents recurrence and is within the organization's control.

**Root cause tree** — Diagram of causes with AND/OR logic.

**Stratification** — Splitting data by potential sources.

**Swim-lane map** — Process map with a lane per role or department.

**VIF** — Variance inflation factor; measures multicollinearity.
