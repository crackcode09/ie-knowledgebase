# Operations Research & Decision Analysis

Decision analysis, optimization, queueing and stochastic models for engineers. This category goes beyond the IISE course catalog.

## Courses

| Course | Topic | Length | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Engineering Decision Making & Risk Management](engineering-decision-making/README.md) | Decision contexts, objectives, measurement scales; AHP, MAUT, swing weighting; group decisions (Borda, Kemeny-Young, majority judgement); aleatory vs epistemic uncertainty, eliciting probabilities; stochastic dominance, decision trees, risk aversion, expected utility; zero-sum and mixed-motive games; incremental and contingency processes, search, secretary problem; EVPI, EVII; risk treatment, potential problem analysis, warnings, ROC curves, risk communication; learning from failures, sensitivity analysis | ~3 days | v1.0 |
