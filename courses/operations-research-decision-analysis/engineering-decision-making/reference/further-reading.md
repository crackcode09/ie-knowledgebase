# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions before buying.

- Keeney, R. L. *Value-Focused Thinking*. Harvard University Press. (Fundamental and means objectives.)
- Keeney, R. L., and Raiffa, H. *Decisions with Multiple Objectives*. Wiley/Cambridge. (MAUT, swing weighting.)
- Clemen, R. T., and Reilly, T. *Making Hard Decisions*. Cengage. (Trees, utility, value of information, elicitation.)
- Saaty, T. L. *The Analytic Hierarchy Process*. McGraw-Hill. (AHP and the random index.)
- Balinski, M., and Laraki, R. *Majority Judgment*. MIT Press.
- Dixit, A., and Nalebuff, B. *Thinking Strategically* / *The Art of Strategy*. Norton. (Game theory.)
- Lindblom, C. E. "The Science of 'Muddling Through'." *Public Administration Review*, 1959.
- Kepner, C. H., and Tregoe, B. B. *The New Rational Manager*. (Potential problem analysis.)
- Fawcett, T. "An introduction to ROC analysis." *Pattern Recognition Letters*, 2006.
- Edmondson, A. C. "Strategies for Learning from Failure." *Harvard Business Review*, 2011.
