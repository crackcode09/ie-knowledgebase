# Analytics, Data Science & AI

Engineering analytics, operational analytics, and applied AI for industrial and systems engineers.

## Courses

| Course | Topic | Length | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Introduction to Engineering Analytics](engineering-analytics/README.md) | Analytics types and process; visualization and dashboards; data models, star schema, SQL; k-means segmentation, ABC classification, logistic and tree classifiers, accuracy measures; regression case study; PCA; forecasting; Big Data technology and enterprise data models; linear-programming optimization; strategy | ~2 days | v1.0 |
| [Advanced Engineering Analytics](advanced-engineering-analytics/README.md) | Business impact of data science; managing and merging multi-source data (Excel + exports); data preparation; descriptive statistics; ANOVA; regression; nonparametric tests and bootstrap; DOE; improving prediction accuracy (CV, features, leakage); dashboards | ~2 days | v1.0 |

| [Human-Centered AI](human-centered-ai/README.md) | Human-centered design foundations; qualitative insight gathering (simulated interviews); from insights to themes; opportunity framing (How might we); bridge to design sprints and rapid prototyping (assumption maps, Wizard of Oz) | ~2 h | v1.0 |
