# Analytics, Data Science & AI

Engineering analytics, operational analytics, and applied AI for industrial and systems engineers.

## Courses

| Course | Topic | Length | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Introduction to Engineering Analytics](engineering-analytics/README.md) | Analytics types and process; visualization and dashboards; data models, star schema, SQL; k-means segmentation, ABC classification, logistic and tree classifiers, accuracy measures; regression case study; PCA; forecasting; Big Data technology and enterprise data models; linear-programming optimization; strategy | ~2 days | draft |
| [Advanced Engineering Analytics](advanced-engineering-analytics/README.md) | Business impact of data science; managing and merging multi-source data (Excel + exports); data preparation; descriptive statistics; ANOVA; regression; nonparametric tests and bootstrap; DOE; improving prediction accuracy (CV, features, leakage); dashboards | ~2 days | draft |
| [Analytics-Based Enterprise Performance Management](analytics-based-epm/README.md) | EPM and ERM; BI vs BA, forecasting vs predictive analytics; ABC/M and chargebacks; strategy maps, balanced scorecards, KPIs vs PIs; customer profitability, cost-to-serve, CLV; unit costing, cost of quality, value-stream costing; driver-based budgets, rolling forecasts, risk-adjusted Monte Carlo, outsourcing; intangible-asset ROI; adoption barriers | 1 day (+ exercises) | draft |
| [Efficient AI](efficient-ai/README.md) | AI foundations (ML, GenAI, rules); problem-first thinking; cross-functional use cases; AI with Lean Six Sigma (PICK chart, DMAIC pilots); risks, limitations, misconceptions (GenAI pilot evaluation); data readiness audit; from ideas to a phased roadmap with business case | ~4 h | draft |
| [Human-Centered AI](human-centered-ai/README.md) | Human-centered design foundations; qualitative insight gathering (simulated interviews); from insights to themes; opportunity framing (How might we); bridge to design sprints and rapid prototyping (assumption maps, Wizard of Oz) | ~2 h | draft |
| [Data Analytics for Business Results](data-analytics-for-business-results/README.md) | Analytics types, lifecycle, MAD dashboards, 12 data-quality dimensions, EDA, Apriori, A/B tests, regression, ML (churn, RFM), storytelling, decision models, biases | 8 h (4 × 2 h) | draft |
