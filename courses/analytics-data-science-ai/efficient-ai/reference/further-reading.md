# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions.

- NIST, *AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, 2023. A public framework for AI risk. Check for the current version.
- Agrawal, A., Gans, J., and Goldfarb, A. *Prediction Machines*. Harvard Business Review Press.
- Provost, F., and Fawcett, T. *Data Science for Business*. O'Reilly.
- Womack, J. P., and Jones, D. T. *Lean Thinking*. Simon & Schuster.
- Davenport, T. H., and Ronanki, R. "Artificial Intelligence for the Real World." *Harvard Business Review*, 2018.

## Notes

- This course deliberately names no AI products or vendors. Capabilities and prices change quickly. Evaluate current tools against your own problem, data and risk criteria.
- All figures in the case are simulated. The cost assumptions ($48/h, intervention $40, late $400, licence and integration costs) are illustrative.
- No statistics on industry-wide AI failure rates are quoted, because published figures vary widely and are hard to verify.
