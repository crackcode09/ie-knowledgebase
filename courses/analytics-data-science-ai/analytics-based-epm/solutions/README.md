## Facilitator notes

- **Module 01:** the outline's first title is quoted verbatim, including its wording.
- **Module 03:** have groups compute one customer's cost-to-serve by hand before revealing the whale curve.
- **Module 04:** ask groups to sort a mixed list of measures into KPIs and PIs.
- **Module 06:** the absorption vs value-stream exercise often surprises finance people. Let them check the arithmetic.
- **Module 07:** emphasise ranges and avoidable cost. Those are the two most common decision errors.
- **Module 09:** collect real barriers from participants and map them to the four types.
