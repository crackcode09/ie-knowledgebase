# ABC/M Model Layout (spreadsheet)

1. **Resources** sheet: general-ledger line, annual cost.
2. **Resource drivers** sheet: resource × activity matrix of shares (each row sums to 100 %); source (interview, time records, m²).
3. **Activities** sheet: activity, cost (= Σ resource × share), driver, driver quantity, rate, level (unit / batch / product / customer / facility), value class (value-adding / required / waste).
4. **Cost objects** sheets: products (units, runs), customers (orders, lines, shipments, returns, calls, visits, specials).
5. **Profitability** sheet: revenue − direct − product-level − customer-level = profit; whale curve.
6. **Checks**: total assigned = total resources; driver quantities reconcile with operational systems.

Keep 10–30 activities; review drivers with the people who do the work; refresh yearly or when processes change.
