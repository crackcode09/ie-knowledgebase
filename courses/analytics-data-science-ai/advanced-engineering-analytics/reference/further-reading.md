# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions first.

- McKinney, W. *Python for Data Analysis*. O'Reilly. Covers pandas, including merging, reshaping and cleaning data.
- Wickham, H. (2014). "Tidy Data." *Journal of Statistical Software*. Explains how to structure data for analysis.
- Montgomery, D. C., Peck, E. A., and Vining, G. G. *Introduction to Linear Regression Analysis*. Wiley.
- Hollander, M., Wolfe, D. A., and Chicken, E. *Nonparametric Statistical Methods*. Wiley.
- Efron, B., and Tibshirani, R. J. *An Introduction to the Bootstrap*. Chapman & Hall.
- James, G., et al. *An Introduction to Statistical Learning*. Springer. Free PDF from the authors. Covers cross-validation, model comparison and overfitting.
- Kuhn, M., and Johnson, K. *Feature Engineering and Selection*. CRC Press. Also free online.
- Knaflic, C. N. *Storytelling with Data*. Wiley.
- Few, S. *Information Dashboard Design*. Analytics Press.
- Documentation for scikit-learn, statsmodels, pandas and Plotly, and for your BI tool. Use these for exact function and feature details.

**Statements in the modules from general knowledge.** Check these before you rely on them:

- descriptions of BI-tool features (joins, relationships, blending);
- the spreadsheet ToolPak functions;
- the VIF rule of thumb (5–10).
