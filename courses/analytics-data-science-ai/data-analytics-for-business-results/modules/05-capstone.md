# Module 05 — Capstone: from question to decision (added)

This module is not in the source outline. It is a project to apply every module to one business question, using the Pinecrest data or your own.

## The brief

Pinecrest's sales director asks: **"Where should we focus next year to grow revenue from existing customers?"** You have one week and the six data sets in `data/`.

## Steps and deliverables

| Step | Module | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Frame | 01 | One sentence each: the decision, who makes it, how success is measured, which analytics type(s) |
| 2. Map | 01 | Order-to-cash map marking where your data comes from (use `templates/use-case-canvas.md`) |
| 3. Check data | 01 | Data-quality scorecard (`templates/data-quality-scorecard.csv`) and what you fixed or flagged |
| 4. Describe | 02 | EDA by branch and segment; two association rules you'd act on |
| 5. Test | 02 | One hypothesis, tested, with effect size and interval |
| 6. Predict | 03 | One model validated against a baseline (sales or churn) |
| 7. Decide | 04 | Expected-value comparison of at least two options, with the assumption that matters most |
| 8. Tell | 04 | A one-page story (`templates/one-page-story.md`) with one chart |

## Self-assessment

| Criterion | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Framing | Starts with the data | Starts with a decision and a measure of success |
| Data quality | Not checked | Checked, scored, and its effect on the conclusion stated |
| Evidence | Point estimates only | Baselines, intervals, holdout results |
| Bias | Not considered | At least one bias named and guarded against |
| Story | Charts in a list | One message, one ask, uncertainty shown |
| Action | "Further analysis needed" | Specific action, owner, date and how it will be measured |
