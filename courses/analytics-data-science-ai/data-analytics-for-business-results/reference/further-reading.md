# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions.

- Eckerson, W. *Performance Dashboards: Measuring, Monitoring, and Managing Your Business*. Wiley. Usually credited with the MAD (Monitor, Analyze, Drill to detail) framework. A web article we opened, [useready.com: MAD framework for dashboard design](https://www.useready.com/blog/mad-framework-for-dashboard-design), uses Monitor–Analyze–Detail in its text but "Metrics, Actions, Data" in its page summary. The sources conflict, so check the book.
- EMC Education Services. *Data Science and Big Data Analytics*. Wiley, 2015. The source of the six-phase data analytics lifecycle in Module 01.
- Chapman, P. et al. *CRISP-DM 1.0: Step-by-step data mining guide*, 2000.
- DAMA UK. *The Six Primary Dimensions for Data Quality Assessment*, 2013. Other lists (DAMA-DMBOK, ISO/IEC 25012) use different dimensions. The 12 in this course are a compiled list, not a standard.
- Agrawal, R. and Srikant, R. "Fast Algorithms for Mining Association Rules." *Proc. VLDB*, 1994.
- Tukey, J. W. *Exploratory Data Analysis*. Addison-Wesley, 1977.
- Stevens, S. S. "On the Theory of Scales of Measurement." *Science*, 1946.
- Mitchell, T. *Machine Learning*. McGraw-Hill, 1997.
- James, G., Witten, D., Hastie, T. and Tibshirani, R. *An Introduction to Statistical Learning*. Springer. Free PDF from the authors.
- Provost, F. and Fawcett, T. *Data Science for Business*. O'Reilly.
- Knaflic, C. N. *Storytelling with Data*. Wiley.
- Tversky, A. and Kahneman, D. "Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases." *Science*, 1974.
- Simon, H. A. *Administrative Behavior*; Klein, G. *Sources of Power*. For bounded rationality and recognition-primed decision making.
- "Systems of record / engagement / insight": industry-analyst terms (for example Geoffrey Moore; Forrester). Their origins were not verified.

All case data and results in this course are simulated.
