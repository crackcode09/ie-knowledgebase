# Role-play Cards for Simulated Interviews

Simulated roles for practice. Interviewees: stay in role; reveal an item only when a question invites it (open questions about real recent events). Do not volunteer solutions.

## P1: CNC operator (2nd shift)

- (pain) Walks to the office PC to look up the latest work instruction revision several times a shift
- (workaround) Keeps a personal notebook of offsets and tricks for difficult part numbers
- (constraint) Wears gloves and cannot use a touchscreen without taking them off
- (need) Wants to know quickly whether a drawing note applies to this revision
- (pain) When the setup expert is off shift, waits or guesses on unfamiliar jobs
- (constraint) Machine area is loud; voice input would be hard to hear

## P2: Maintenance technician

- (pain) Searches three places for the last repair history of a machine
- (goal) Wants to fix a breakdown right the first time instead of trial and error
- (workaround) Texts photos of faults to a retired colleague who knows the old machines
- (pain) Planned maintenance is skipped whenever there is a breakdown elsewhere
- (need) Needs the fault code, the manual page and past fixes together in one place
- (constraint) Hands are often oily; phone stays in the pocket during repairs

## P3: Production planner

- (pain) Copies orders from the ERP into a spreadsheet every morning to build the schedule
- (pain) Does not trust the system lead times so adds own buffer to every order
- (goal) Wants to see the impact of a rush order on other customers before saying yes
- (workaround) Calls the floor to check machine status because the system is not up to date
- (pain) Half the day goes to expediting instead of planning ahead
- (need) Needs to know why a forecast changed before acting on it

## P4: Customer service agent

- (pain) Toggles between four screens to answer one order status question
- (need) Wants a quick answer on technical questions without waiting for engineering
- (pain) Spends a long time finding the right spec sheet for a customer question
- (goal) Wants to give customers a delivery date she can stand behind
- (workaround) Keeps a shared document of answers to common technical questions
- (constraint) Must answer phone calls within a few minutes; no time for long searches

## P5: Accounts payable clerk

- (pain) Re-keys invoice lines from PDF into the ERP by hand
- (pain) Reconciles price differences between PO and invoice line by line
- (need) Wants exceptions flagged with the reason so she can act quickly
- (goal) Wants to stop month-end overtime
- (workaround) Keeps a personal list of suppliers whose invoices always have unit-of-measure problems
- (pain) Would not approve a payment suggested by a system without seeing why it matched

## P6: Quality engineer

- (pain) Writing an 8D report means hunting through emails for the complaint history
- (need) Wants to see all similar past complaints and their root causes in one view
- (pain) Investigations are interrupted by daily containment firefights
- (goal) Wants to spot recurring problems before the customer does
- (workaround) Exports data from three systems and joins them in a spreadsheet
- (pain) Does not trust a defect rate unless she can see the raw inspection records

## P7: Shift supervisor

- (pain) Spends the first hour of the shift chasing what happened on the previous shift
- (need) Wants handover notes that are complete and quick to read
- (pain) Only two people can set up the new machine; absences stop production
- (goal) Wants the team to solve problems without escalating everything
- (workaround) Uses a whiteboard because the system board is never current
- (constraint) No time during the shift for training on new software

## P8: Warehouse picker

- (pain) Walks back to the desk when a pick location is wrong in the system
- (constraint) Scanner gun is the only device carried; screen is small
- (workaround) Remembers where fast movers really are instead of trusting the system
- (pain) Rush orders interrupt planned picking waves
- (need) Wants to know about a shortage before walking to the location
- (goal) Wants fewer wasted trips across the warehouse
