# Cheat Sheet

**HCD principles:** understand users, tasks and environment · involve users throughout · evaluation drives design · iterate · whole experience · multidisciplinary team.

**Interview rules:** last-time stories · open, non-leading questions · ask why · watch for workarounds · separate observations from interpretations · consent and anonymity · no pitching.

**Note types:** pain · need · goal · workaround · constraint.

**Synthesis:** diverge (all notes up) → converge (silent affinity grouping → themes named as sentences) → weigh by breadth across roles and by workarounds.

**HMW statement:** "How might we [outcome] for [user] in [situation]?" with no technology words; not too broad, not too narrow.

**Prioritize:** desirability · feasibility · viability · AI fit (scores 1–5, weighted).

**Assumption map:** importance (if wrong) × evidence (known). Test high-importance, low-evidence assumptions first.

**Cheapest prototypes:** Wizard of Oz · concierge · paper or cardboard · data audit · fake door. Set success criteria before testing.
