# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions.

- ISO 9241-210, *Ergonomics of human-system interaction — Human-centred design for interactive systems*. The principles in Module 01 paraphrase the course author's understanding of this standard. Check the standard's current text.
- Design Council (UK), "Double Diamond" design process model.
- Knapp, J., Zeratsky, J., and Kowitz, B. *Sprint*. Simon & Schuster. (Five-day design sprint.)
- IDEO.org, *The Field Guide to Human-Centered Design*.
- Fitzpatrick, R. *The Mom Test*. (Asking about past behaviour rather than opinions.)
- Bland, D. J., and Osterwalder, A. *Testing Business Ideas*. Wiley. (Assumption mapping, prototype experiments.)
- Holtzblatt, K., and Beyer, H. *Contextual Design*. Morgan Kaufmann.

All case data, interview notes, scores and test results in this course are simulated.
