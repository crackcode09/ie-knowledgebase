# Interview Guide (20–30 minutes)

**Before:** explain the purpose ("to understand your work, not to evaluate you"), ask for consent to take notes, confirm notes are anonymous.

## Warm-up (2 min)
- What does a typical shift or day look like for you?

## The last time (10–15 min)
- Tell me about the last time [task / problem area] was slow or frustrating. What happened, step by step?
- Where did you get the information you needed? How long did it take?
- What did you do when it wasn't available? (look for workarounds)
- Who do you go to when you're stuck? What happens when they're not there?

## Deeper (5–10 min)
- Why was that hard? What happened next?
- Show me how you do that now (observe, if possible).
- What would have to be true for this to be easy?

## Close (2 min)
- Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?
- Who else should we talk to?

**Rules:** specific past events · open questions · no leading · no pitching · capture words and observations separately from your interpretations.
