# Module 02 — Qualitative Insight Gathering

Best practices for interviewing and observing frontline, operational and knowledge workers with an HCD mindset.

## Objectives

- Plan and run short, open interviews and observations.
- Avoid leading questions and solution pitches.
- Practise through simulated interviews with frontline and operational roles.

## Interview and observe

- **Interviews** reveal goals, frustrations and reasons.
- **Observation** (going to where the work happens, "gemba", or contextual inquiry) reveals what people actually do, including workarounds they no longer notice.

Use both. What people say and what they do often differ.

## Best practices

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Ask about **specific past events**: "Tell me about the last time you…" | Ask hypotheticals: "Would you use an AI that…?" |
| Ask open questions: what, how, walk me through… | Ask yes/no or leading questions: "Isn't it annoying that…?" |
| Dig deeper: "Why was that? What happened next?" | Accept the first answer |
| Watch for **workarounds**: notebooks, spreadsheets, whiteboards, phone photos | Dismiss workarounds as bad habits. They are evidence of unmet needs |
| Capture exact words and observations, separately from your interpretation | Write conclusions during the interview |
| Explain the purpose; get consent; keep notes anonymous | Use what people say against them |
| Talk to several roles, including frontline and knowledge workers | Interview only managers about other people's work |
| Keep it short: 20–30 minutes, in their workplace | Pitch your solution |

Interview guide: `templates/interview-guide.md`. Note sheet: `templates/note-sheet.csv` (one observation per row; type = pain, need, goal, workaround or constraint).

## Simulated interviews

Work in threes: an **interviewer**, an **interviewee** playing a role from `data/roleplay_cards.md`, and a **note-taker**. Rotate every 8 minutes.

The note-taker records one observation per sticky note or row, tagged with its type. After three rounds, compare your notes with the example notes in `data/interview_notes.csv`. These are 48 simulated notes from eight roles: CNC operator, maintenance technician, production planner, customer service agent, accounts payable clerk, quality engineer, shift supervisor and warehouse picker.

| Type | Count in the example | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | 19 | "Walks to the office PC to look up the latest work instruction revision several times a shift" |
| Need | 8 | "Needs the fault code, the manual page and past fixes together in one place" |
| Goal | 7 | "Wants to fix a breakdown right the first time" |
| Workaround | 8 | "Texts photos of faults to a retired colleague who knows the old machines" |
| Constraint | 6 | "Wears gloves and cannot use a touchscreen without taking them off" |

All interview content in this course is simulated for teaching.
