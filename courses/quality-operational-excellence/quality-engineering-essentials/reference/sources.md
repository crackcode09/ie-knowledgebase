## Claims that rest on general practice rather than a listed source

Check these before relying on them; a reviewer should either attach a source or confirm the module labels the claim as a convention.

- the gauge R&R acceptance guidelines as applied to this case (03);
- the 1.5σ shift convention (01, 03);
- the 8-wastes list (01);
- the kappa bar the course trains to, 0.9 / 0.7 (03), stated in the module as the course's own bar beside the AIAG baseline;
- the awareness, understanding, acceptance, commitment sequence (06), stated as the course's own summary with Kotter cited as background.
