# Formula Sheet

## Totals and categories

- CoQ = Prevention + Appraisal + Internal failure + External failure
- Cost of good quality (conformance) = P + A
- Cost of poor quality (nonconformance) = IF + EF

## Measurement bases and ratios

- CoQ % of sales = CoQ / sales
- CoQ per unit = CoQ / units shipped
- CoQ per labor hour = CoQ / direct labor hours
- Failure share = (IF + EF) / CoQ
- Prevention share = P / CoQ

## Valuation

- Scrap = accumulated material + labor + overhead to the point of scrap − salvage
- Rework = labor + materials + extra machine time
- Downgrade = full selling price − downgrade price
- Lost bottleneck capacity = quality-caused downtime hours × contribution margin per bottleneck hour
- Work-sampling estimate = share of observations × number of people × loaded annual cost

## Quadratic quality loss

- L(y) = k(y − T)², with k = (loss at the specification limit) / (half-tolerance)²
- Expected loss per unit = k[σ² + (μ − T)²]

## Project economics

- Annual saving = annual driver cost × expected reduction − added recurring cost
- Payback (months) = 12 × one-time cost / annual saving
- First-year ROI = (annual saving − one-time cost) / one-time cost
- Net saving = Δ(IF + EF + A) − ΔP
