# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions first.

- Campanella, J. (ed.). *Principles of Quality Costs*. ASQ Quality Press. The classic guide to the PAF model and quality-cost programs.
- Feigenbaum, A. V. *Total Quality Control*. McGraw-Hill. An early source of the quality-cost categories.
- Juran, J. M., and De Feo, J. A. *Juran's Quality Handbook*. McGraw-Hill. Includes a chapter on the costs of poor quality.
- Crosby, P. B. *Quality Is Free*. McGraw-Hill. The source of the "price of conformance / nonconformance" framing.
- Schiffauerova, A., and Thomson, V. (2006). "A review of research on cost of quality models and best practices." *International Journal of Quality & Reliability Management*. Compares PAF, process cost, activity-based and opportunity-cost models.
- Taguchi, G., Chowdhury, S., and Wu, Y. *Taguchi's Quality Engineering Handbook*. Wiley. Covers the quality loss function.
- ISO 10014: guidance on realizing financial and economic benefits from quality management. Check the current edition.
