# Cost-of-Quality Element Checklist

Tick the elements that apply, name the data source and owner, and keep the category rules consistent from period to period.

## Prevention
- [ ] Quality planning and engineering (control plans, process design for quality)
- [ ] Design reviews, DFMEA / PFMEA
- [ ] Training in quality methods and standard work
- [ ] Supplier selection and development
- [ ] Error-proofing design and installation
- [ ] Preventive / predictive maintenance of quality-critical equipment
- [ ] Process control system design (SPC setup)
- [ ] Quality improvement project time

## Appraisal (detection)
- [ ] Incoming / receiving inspection and test
- [ ] In-process and first-article inspection
- [ ] Final inspection and product test
- [ ] Calibration and maintenance of measuring equipment
- [ ] Product, process and system audits
- [ ] Test materials and destroyed samples
- [ ] Supplier surveillance (checking)

## Internal failure
- [ ] Scrap (valued at accumulated cost minus salvage)
- [ ] Rework and repair
- [ ] Re-inspection and re-test
- [ ] Failure investigation, MRB
- [ ] Supplier-reject handling
- [ ] Downgrading
- [ ] Quality-caused downtime (if booked)

## External failure
- [ ] Warranty claims
- [ ] Returns processing and replacement
- [ ] Field service
- [ ] Complaint handling
- [ ] Concessions, credits, penalties
- [ ] Recalls and product liability

## Hidden (estimate separately)
- [ ] Lost sales / customers
- [ ] Lost bottleneck capacity
- [ ] Engineering and management firefighting
- [ ] Expediting and premium freight
- [ ] Excess inventory held for yield loss
- [ ] Overtime to make up losses
