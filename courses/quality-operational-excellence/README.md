# Quality & Operational Excellence

Quality fundamentals, cost of quality, quality engineering, and statistical quality control.

## Courses

| Course | Topic | Length | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Statistical Quality Control](statistical-quality-control/README.md) | Descriptive statistics; common vs special cause; X̄–R, I–MR, p, c and short-run charts; Cp/Cpk; acceptance sampling: risks, OC curves, AOQ/AOQL, ASN, ATI, ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 use | ~3 days | draft |
| [Cost of Quality](cost-of-quality/README.md) | CoQ models (PAF, process cost, activity-based); prevention, appraisal, internal and external failure; hidden costs and quality loss; cost assessments; cost drivers; project selection and savings; CoQ reports; Excel tracker | ~2 days | draft |
| [Quality Engineering Essentials](quality-engineering-essentials/README.md) | DMAIC quality toolkit: charter, 8 wastes, basic statistics, distribution ID, data cleansing, gauge R&R and attribute MSA, baseline/DPMO, SPC with transformations and stage variables, capability, root cause (fishbone, 5-Why, stratification, regression), FMEA, reliability, error-proofing, pilots, change management, control plans, 5S, standard work | ~39 h | draft |
