## What was taken from the IISE catalog, and what was not

**Used:** the publicly listed course titles and topic outlines of the IISE Training Center catalog, as a checklist of subjects an industrial-engineering curriculum should cover. The category names and the course titles in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) follow that catalog so readers can see what is covered and what is missing. Some courses also follow the order of topics in a public course outline and say so in their README.

**Not used:** no IISE course materials, slides, handouts, workbooks, lesson text, exercises, exams, datasets, figures or instructor notes. Nobody who built these courses had access to that material while writing. Every lesson, worked example, dataset, figure, script and solution in this repository was written independently, with AI assistance, and is original. Where a subject has an official licensed data card or tables (MODAPTS, MTM-1), the course uses its own simplified teaching version and says so; the official data is not reproduced.

A list of topics is a statement of what a subject contains; using it as a coverage map is, we believe, fair and lawful. If a rights holder disagrees about a specific page, see "Reporting a problem" below.

## AI-assisted content and verification status

The courses were written with substantial help from AI language models, guided by contributors. Every course carries a status in the catalog tables:

- `draft`: written, not yet checked by a reviewer. Figures, formulas and references may be wrong.
- `verified (Reviewer, YYYY-MM-DD)`: a named reviewer has completed [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md).

Until a course is verified, treat it as a study guide to check against primary sources. The site shows the status on every course card and lesson.
