# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current edition first.

- Minto, B. *The Pyramid Principle: Logic in Writing and Thinking*. The source of the answer-first, pyramid and situation–complication–resolution structures in Session 03.
- Tufte, E. R. *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*. Graphics Press. The classic book on clear, honest charts.
- Knaflic, C. N. *Storytelling with Data*. Wiley. A practical guide to decluttering charts and focusing attention, written for business audiences.
- Few, S. *Show Me the Numbers*. Analytics Press. Choosing tables and graphs for quantitative business data.
- Alley, M. *The Craft of Scientific Presentations*. Springer. Covers the sentence-headline (action title) slide design for engineers and scientists.
- Reynolds, G. *Presentation Zen*. New Riders. Slide simplicity and delivery.
- Williams, J. M. *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace*. Pearson. Clear sentence-level writing.
- Flesch, R. *The Art of Readable Writing*. The origin of the Flesch reading-ease score.
