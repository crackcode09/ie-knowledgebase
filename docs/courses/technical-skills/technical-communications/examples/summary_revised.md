We cut scrap on grinding Line 3 by more than half, from 4.5% to 2.0% of parts, which saves about $86,000 a year. Most of the scrap came from parts ground oversize or burned, and both problems traced back to two causes: the coolant mix drifted well away from its target, and operators dressed the grinding wheel at very different intervals. We installed a pump that holds the coolant mix steady, set the machine to dress the wheel every 40 parts, and added a gauge that checks each part and corrects the machine automatically. Scrap has stayed near 2% for 13 weeks. We ask you to approve $38,000 to apply the same fix to Lines 1, 2 and 4, which we expect to save about $250,000 a year and pay back in about two months.
