# Glossary

**Bootstrap** — Resampling the data with replacement to estimate the uncertainty of a statistic.

**Central limit theorem** — Means of samples tend toward a normal distribution as n grows.

**Chi-square distribution** — Distribution of scaled sample variances; used for SD intervals, goodness of fit, contingency tables.

**Confidence interval** — Range from a method that captures the true parameter at the stated rate.

**Correlation (r)** — Strength and direction of a linear relationship, −1 to +1.

**Excess kurtosis** — Tail heaviness relative to the normal (0 = normal).

**F distribution** — Distribution of a ratio of two variances.

**Hypergeometric** — Counts of defectives in a sample drawn without replacement from a finite lot.

**Masking** — A strong variable hides the effect of a weaker one in simple correlations.

**MTBF** — Mean time between failures.

**OC curve** — Probability a sampling plan accepts lots of each quality level.

**Operational definition** — Precise description of how a measurement is made.

**p-value** — Probability, if H₀ is true, of a result at least as extreme as observed.

**Prediction interval** — Range for a single future observation.

**Risk (α)** — Probability of a Type I error; 1 − confidence level.

**Skewness** — Asymmetry of a distribution.

**Standard error** — Standard deviation of a statistic, e.g., s/√n for a mean.

**Student's t** — Distribution for standardized means when σ is estimated.

**Type I / Type II error** — Rejecting a true H₀ / failing to reject a false H₀.
