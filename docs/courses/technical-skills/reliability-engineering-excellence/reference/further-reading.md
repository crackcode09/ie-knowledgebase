# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions, authorship details and the current status of each standard were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions first.

## Books and reports

- Moubray, J. *Reliability-Centred Maintenance (RCM II)*. The standard practitioner text on RCM.
- Nowlan, F. S., and Heap, H. F. *Reliability-Centered Maintenance* (1978 report for the US Department of Defense). The origin of RCM and of the failure-pattern findings mentioned in Module 00.
- Abernethy, R. B. *The New Weibull Handbook*. A practical guide to Weibull analysis, including suspensions and small samples.
- O'Connor, P. D. T., and Kleyner, A. *Practical Reliability Engineering*. Wiley. A broad reliability-engineering text covering statistics, design, testing and growth.
- Ebeling, C. E. *An Introduction to Reliability and Maintainability Engineering*. Covers the formulas behind Modules 02, 05, 06 and 09.
- Nakajima, S. *Introduction to TPM*. The foundational TPM text.
- Smith, R., and Mobley, R. K. *Rules of Thumb for Maintenance and Reliability Engineers*.
- Barringer, H. P. Papers on life-cycle cost and Weibull analysis.

## Standards

- **SAE JA1011 / JA1012:** criteria for, and a guide to, RCM processes.
- **ISO 55000 series:** asset management.
- **ISO 17359 and related ISO standards:** condition monitoring and diagnostics of machines.
- **IEC 60812:** FMEA/FMECA.
- **IEC 61649:** Weibull analysis.
