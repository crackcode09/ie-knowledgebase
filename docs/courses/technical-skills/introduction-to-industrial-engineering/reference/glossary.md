# Glossary

**Allowance** — Time added to normal time for personal needs, fatigue and unavoidable delays.

**Anthropometry** — Measurement of human body dimensions for design.

**ECRS** — Eliminate, Combine, Rearrange, Simplify.

**Ergonomics (human factors)** — Designing work to fit human capabilities and limits.

**From–to chart** — Matrix of flows (trips, loads) between departments.

**Gainsharing** — Plant-wide sharing of productivity gains with employees.

**Industrial engineering** — Design, improvement and installation of integrated systems of people, materials, information, equipment and energy.

**Job analysis** — Study of a job's tasks and the knowledge, skills, abilities and other characteristics it requires.

**Job evaluation** — Determining the relative worth of jobs (ranking, classification, factor comparison, point-factor).

**Learning curve** — Reduction in unit time with cumulative output; constant percentage per doubling.

**Lifting index (LI)** — Load ÷ NIOSH recommended weight limit.

**Load–distance** — Σ flow × distance; measure of layout efficiency.

**Motion economy** — Principles for efficient, less fatiguing hand and body motions.

**Normal time** — Observed time × performance rating.

**Performance rating** — Analyst's judgment of observed pace relative to normal pace.

**Point-factor method** — Job evaluation by weighted compensable factors.

**Predetermined motion time system (PMTS)** — Standard times for basic motions, summed to build task times.

**Process chart** — Record of a process using operation, transport, inspection, delay and storage symbols.

**Standard time** — Normal time plus allowances; time for a qualified worker at normal pace.

**Therbligs** — Gilbreths' basic elements of hand motion.

**Time study** — Stopwatch measurement of a task to set a standard.

**Two-hand chart** — Side-by-side record of left- and right-hand motions.

**Wage line** — Regression of pay on job-evaluation points.

**Work sampling** — Random instantaneous observations to estimate the share of time in activities.
