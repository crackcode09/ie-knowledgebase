# Module 8 — Labor Skills, Competencies and Job Analysis

**Time:** 60 min · **Day 3**

## Learning objectives

1. Conduct a job analysis and write a job description.
2. Build a skills and competency matrix for cross-training.

## 1. Job analysis

The systematic study of a job to identify its **tasks, duties and responsibilities**, and the **knowledge, skills, abilities and other characteristics (KSAOs)** needed. It's the foundation for job descriptions, hiring, training, performance standards, job evaluation (Module 9) and pay (Module 10).

**Methods:** observation, interviews, questionnaires, work logs, critical incidents, task analysis by subject-matter experts.

### Job description — Kit Packer (outline)

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Pick, inspect, pack and label service kits to standard, on time, without errors |
| Main tasks (% time) | Pick parts (40%), inspect and bag (25%), seal, label and box (15%), replenish bench supplies (10%), 5S and records (10%) |
| Standards | 58 kits/hour at standard; 99.8% kit accuracy |
| KSAOs | Read part numbers and pick lists; basic scanner use; visual inspection; lift up to 14 kg with assist |
| Working conditions | Standing; adjustable bench; lifting assisted |
| Reports to | Team leader |

Write tasks as **action verb + object + purpose**, measurable where possible.

## 2. Skills and competencies

- **Skill**: ability to perform a specific task (operate a heat sealer).
- **Competency**: broader combination of knowledge, skills and behaviors needed for success (quality mindset, problem solving).

### Skills (cross-training) matrix

| Person | Pick | Inspect | Seal/label | Replenish | Train others |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | ● | ● | ● | ◐ | ● |
| B | ● | ◐ | ● | ○ | ○ |
| C | ● | ● | ◐ | ● | ○ |
| D | ◐ | ○ | ● | ● | ○ |

● can train others · ◐ can do independently · ○ in training / not trained

**Use:** spot single points of failure (only A can train), plan cross-training so every task has at least three qualified people, support flexible staffing when demand changes.

## Check yourself

Why should job analysis focus on the job, not the current person in it?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

So descriptions, evaluation and pay reflect the job's requirements consistently, not one individual's strengths, habits or performance.
</details>
