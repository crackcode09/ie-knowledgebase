# Module 2 — Work Methods Analysis

**Time:** 90 min

## Learning objectives

1. Define a process and document it with process charts.
2. Identify inefficiencies using a structured questioning technique.
3. Develop and evaluate an improved method.

## 1. Methods analysis steps

1. **Select** the work to study (high cost, high volume, bottleneck, safety issue, complaints).
2. **Record** the present method (process chart, flow diagram, video).
3. **Examine** critically — question every step.
4. **Develop** a better method (with the people who do it).
5. **Measure** it (Modules 4–5) and **install** it with training and standard work.
6. **Maintain** and audit.

## 2. Flow process chart

ASME standard symbols (from the Gilbreths' process charts):

| Symbol | Activity |
|---|---|
| ○ | Operation — something is changed or created |
| ⇨ | Transport — moved from one place to another |
| □ | Inspection — checked for quality or quantity |
| D | Delay — waiting |
| ▽ | Storage — held under control |

### Case — packing one kit (present method)

| # | Activity | Symbol | Distance (m) | Time (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get bag and label from shelf behind bench | ⇨ | 3 | 6 |
| 2 | Walk to bin rack, pick 8 parts | ⇨ ○ | 8 | 22 |
| 3 | Return to bench | ⇨ | 8 | (incl.) |
| 4 | Inspect and bag parts | □ ○ | | 14 |
| 5 | Seal and label | ○ | | 6 |
| 6 | Wait for full box to be removed | D | | (varies) |
| 7 | Place in box | ○ | | 3 |

Summary: 4 operations, 3 transports (19 m per kit), 1 inspection, 1 delay.

## 3. Critical examination: questioning and ECRS

For every step ask **What? Why? Where? When? Who? How?** — and then apply **ECRS**:

| Principle | Question | Case idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Eliminate** | Can we drop it? | Walking to bins: present parts at the bench |
| **Combine** | Can steps be done together? | Inspect while bagging (one handling) |
| **Rearrange** | Better sequence or place? | Label printer at the bench; boxes on a roller lane that removes itself |
| **Simplify** | Easier way? | Kit tray with one cavity per part (also error-proofs counting) |

**Proposed method:** gravity-fed bins in an arc at the bench, kit tray, label printer at the bench, roller conveyor for full boxes. Walking drops from 19 m to ~0; the waiting delay disappears; picking errors drop (tray shows a missing part).

## 4. Other charts

| Chart | Use |

## Check yourself

Why record the present method before designing a new one?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

You can't improve what you don't understand; the record exposes the waste, gives a baseline to measure against, and earns credibility with the people doing the work.
</details>
