# Glossary

- **Appraisal (detection) cost:** the cost of measuring and checking conformance.
- **Cost driver:** a failure type or cause whose occurrences create quality cost.
- **Cost of conformance / nonconformance:** what it costs to do things right / what is lost by doing them wrong (process cost model).
- **Cost of good quality / poor quality:** prevention plus appraisal / internal plus external failure.
- **Cost of quality (CoQ):** the total of prevention, appraisal, internal failure and external failure costs.
- **External failure cost:** the cost of defects found after delivery.
- **Hard saving:** a saving that removes cost from the books.
- **Hidden cost:** a quality-related cost not captured in accounts (lost sales, lost capacity, firefighting).
- **Internal failure cost:** the cost of defects found before delivery.
- **Measurement base:** the denominator used to normalize CoQ (sales, units, labor hours).
- **MRB:** material review board. The group that decides the disposition of nonconforming material.
- **PAF model:** the prevention–appraisal–failure model.
- **Prevention cost:** the cost of activities that stop defects from occurring.
- **Quadratic loss function:** a model in which loss grows with the square of the deviation from target.
- **Soft saving:** freed capacity or time that becomes a real saving only if redeployed.
- **Work sampling:** estimating time shares from random observations.
