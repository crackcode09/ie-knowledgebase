# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions first.

- Montgomery, D. C. *Introduction to Statistical Quality Control*. Wiley.
- AIAG. *Measurement Systems Analysis (MSA) Reference Manual*. The source of the common gauge R&R guidelines (10% / 30%, ndc ≥ 5) and of the ANOVA method.
- AIAG and VDA. *FMEA Handbook*. Introduces action-priority (AP) tables as an alternative to RPN.
- George, M. L., et al. *The Lean Six Sigma Pocket Toolbook*. McGraw-Hill. Concise DMAIC tool reference.
- Womack, J. P., and Jones, D. T. *Lean Thinking*. The source of the five Lean principles.
- Shingo, S. *Zero Quality Control: Source Inspection and the Poka-Yoke System*. Covers error-proofing.
- Kotter, J. P. *Leading Change*. Harvard Business Review Press. Covers change leadership.
- Landis, J. R., and Koch, G. G. (1977). "The measurement of observer agreement for categorical data." *Biometrics*. A widely cited source for kappa interpretation bands. Note that the bands in Module 03 are a stricter quality-practice convention.
- NIST/SEMATECH *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*. Free online.

**Points in the modules that rest on general knowledge rather than verified sources** (check them before citing):

- the gauge R&R acceptance guidelines;
- the kappa bands;
- the 1.5σ shift convention;
- the five Lean principles and the 8-wastes acronym;
- the change-management framing.
