# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current edition first.

## Books and handbooks

- Montgomery, D. C. *Introduction to Statistical Quality Control*. Wiley. The standard text for every topic in this course.
- Wheeler, D. J., and Chambers, D. S. *Understanding Statistical Process Control*. SPC Press.
- Grant, E. L., and Leavenworth, R. S. *Statistical Quality Control*. McGraw-Hill. Covers sampling plans and OC curves in depth.
- Schilling, E. G., and Neubauer, D. V. *Acceptance Sampling in Quality Control*. CRC Press.
- NIST/SEMATECH *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*, chapter 6 (Process or Product Monitoring and Control). Free online.

## Standards (purchase current editions; not reproduced here)

- ANSI/ASQ Z1.4: sampling procedures and tables for inspection by attributes.
- ANSI/ASQ Z1.9: sampling procedures and tables for inspection by variables.
- ISO 2859 series: the international counterpart for attribute sampling.
- ISO 7870 series: control charts.
- ISO 22514 series: process capability and performance.
