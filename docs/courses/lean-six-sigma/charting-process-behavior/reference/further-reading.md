# Further Reading

Primary and classic sources for the body of knowledge in this course. Editions listed are the commonly cited ones; check for newer editions.

| Source | Why read it |
|---|---|
| Shewhart, W. A. *Economic Control of Quality of Manufactured Product*. Van Nostrand, 1931. | The original theory of control charts |
| Shewhart, W. A. *Statistical Method from the Viewpoint of Quality Control*. USDA Graduate School, 1939 (edited by W. E. Deming). | Shewhart's own explanation of the method and the improvement cycle |
| Western Electric Co. *Statistical Quality Control Handbook*. 1956. | Origin of the zone tests (Module 7); practical pattern interpretation |
| Deming, W. E. *Out of the Crisis*. MIT CAES, 1986. | Common vs special causes, management's role, the funnel |
| Deming, W. E. *The New Economics for Industry, Government, Education*. MIT CAES, 1993. | System of profound knowledge, funnel experiment |
| Wheeler, D. J. *Understanding Variation: The Key to Managing Chaos*. SPC Press, 1993 (2nd ed. 2000). | Short, readable; XmR charts for managers |
| Wheeler, D. J., & Chambers, D. S. *Understanding Statistical Process Control*. SPC Press, 2nd ed. 1992. | Complete practitioner's reference |
| Wheeler, D. J. *Normality and the Process Behavior Chart*. SPC Press, 2000. | Why charts don't need normality (Module 13) |
| Montgomery, D. C. *Introduction to Statistical Quality Control*. Wiley (many editions). | University textbook; EWMA, CUSUM, multivariate (Module 14) |
| NIST/SEMATECH *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*, Section 6.3 "Univariate and Multivariate Control Charts". https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ | Free, authoritative online reference |
| AIAG. *Statistical Process Control (SPC) Reference Manual*, 2nd ed. 2005. | Automotive industry conventions for charts and capability |
| AIAG. *Measurement Systems Analysis (MSA) Reference Manual*, 4th ed. 2010. | Gauge R&R (Module 1) |
