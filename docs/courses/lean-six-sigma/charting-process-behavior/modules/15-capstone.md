# Module 15 — Capstone Project

**Time:** 4–8 hours over 2–4 weeks

Apply the full method to a real process. If you have no process at work, use a personal one (commute time, daily steps, time to first coffee) — the method is the same.

## Deliverable

A short report (2–4 pages, or one slide per step) with charts.

## Steps and checklist

| # | Step | Module | Done? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State the process, the customer, and the question you want answered | 0 | ☐ |
| 2 | Write an operational definition; check measurement resolution and repeatability | 1 | ☐ |
| 3 | Collect ≥ 25 time-ordered points with stage variables (machine, shift, lot…) | 1, 11 | ☐ |
| 4 | Plot a run chart and a histogram | 2, 4 | ☐ |
| 5 | Pick the right chart (XmR, X̄–R, p/np/c/u) and justify the choice; design subgroups if used | 6, 8, 9 | ☐ |
| 6 | Compute limits from a baseline; apply detection rules chosen in advance | 5–7 | ☐ |
| 7 | Investigate every signal; record what you found (or didn't) | 7 | ☐ |
| 8 | Stratify by at least one stage variable | 11 | ☐ |
| 9 | If predictable and a spec exists, compute capability; check shape | 12, 13 | ☐ |
| 10 | Classify the process: ideal / threshold / brink of chaos / chaos | 5 | ☐ |
| 11 | Recommend action: remove special causes, change the system, or leave alone | 5, 10 | ☐ |
| 12 | Write the reaction plan and post the chart | 7 | ☐ |

## Scoring rubric (self or peer review)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operational definition | Missing | Vague | Repeatable by another person |
| Chart selection | Wrong chart | Right chart, no reasoning | Right chart with reasoning |
| Limits | Specs used as limits, or limits from overall s | Correct but no baseline logic | Correct, baseline frozen and extended |
| Signal interpretation | Not attempted | Signals listed | Signals investigated, findings recorded |
| Capability | Computed on unstable process | Computed, preconditions not checked | Preconditions checked, right method |
| Recommendation | None | Generic | Specific and matched to the process state |

10–12 points: ready to lead charting on an improvement team.
