# Glossary

**%C&A (percent complete and accurate)** — Share of work received by a step that can be used without correction, added information or clarification. Rolled %C&A = product across steps.

**Affinity diagram** — Groups verbatim statements into themes; headers state the underlying needs.

**As-is / to-be map** — Current process as it actually runs / designed future process.

**BVA (business-value-added)** — Required today but not valued by the customer; also "necessary non-value-added."

**CTQ (critical to quality)** — Measurable characteristic with a target and limit, traced to a customer need.

**CTQ tree** — Tree from customer need → drivers → CTQs.

**Handoff** — Transfer of work between people or groups; usually a queue.

**Kano model** — Basic, performance and delighter needs; delighters become basics over time.

**Lead time** — Elapsed time from start trigger to end point.

**NVA (non-value-added)** — Waste: can be removed.

**Operational definition** — Precise description of how a measurement is made.

**Process time / work time** — Time actually spent working on the item.

**SIPOC** — Suppliers, Inputs, Process, Outputs, Customers.

**Spaghetti diagram** — Floor plan with the path of material, information or people.

**Swim-lane map** — Process map with one lane per role or group.

**VA (value-added)** — Customer would pay for it; changes form, fit or function; right first time.

**Value stream map (VSM)** — Material and information flow with process data and a lead-time timeline.

**VOC (voice of the customer)** — Customers' needs and expectations, in their words, and the data describing them.
