# Module 11 — Skill Check and Capstone

**Time:** 45 min + capstone

## Part A — Quiz

1. You need to agree scope with a sponsor before any analysis. Which map?
2. A process has 12 steps across 5 departments with 8 handoffs. Which map exposes this best?
3. Classify: (a) re-keying an order from email into ERP; (b) heat-treating a shaft; (c) a regulatory label check.
4. A part travels Receiving → Lab → Office → Lab → Bench → Lab → Shipping. What does the spaghetti diagram suggest?
5. Four steps have %C&A of 90%, 80%, 95%, 70%. Rolled %C&A?
6. A delighter from five years ago is now expected. What happened, and what does it mean for VOC?
7. Write a CTQ with an operational definition for the need "invoices should be right."
8. 200 customers state the longest acceptable delivery time; the 10th percentile is 4 days. What does a 4-day upper limit mean?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. SIPOC.
2. Swim-lane map.
3. (a) NVA; (b) VA; (c) BVA.
4. Backtracking around the lab and a trip to the office — candidates: co-locate bench and lab, remove the office trip (electronic decision).
5. 0.90 × 0.80 × 0.95 × 0.70 = 0.479 → 48%.
6. Expectations migrated (Kano): delighter → basic. VOC must be refreshed regularly.
7. E.g., "Invoice accuracy: share of invoices with no customer dispute or credit note within 60 days of issue; target ≥ 99.5%."
8. About 90% of customers would accept it (it's within their stated tolerance); about 10% need faster.
</details>

## Part B — Capstone

Map a process you work in (or the order-to-ship process for a small company):

1. SIPOC with start and end triggers.
2. As-is swim-lane map, verified by walking the process; count handoffs.
3. Value-added flow chart with work and wait times; compute VA ÷ lead time.
4. Spaghetti diagram for the physical or information path; measure it.
5. Current-state VSM with %C&A and rolled %C&A.
6. VOC: at least 15 verbatims → affinity diagram → CTQ tree with operational definitions.
7. One CTQ turned into a specification using customer data.
8. Future-state VSM that meets the specification; list of changes.

Templates: `templates/`. Rubric: each item scored 0 (missing) / 1 (present) / 2 (verified with data and traceable to the customer). 13+ of 16: ready to lead mapping on an improvement team.
