# Glossary

**Affinity diagram** — Groups verbatim statements into themes with need headers.

**Better / worse coefficients** — Kano summary: share of respondents whose satisfaction rises if a feature is present / falls if absent.

**Cause-and-effect (C&E) matrix** — Scores process inputs by their weighted impact on customer CTQs.

**CTQ** — Critical-to-quality characteristic: measurable, with target and limit, traced to a need.

**CTQ tree** — Need → drivers → CTQs.

**Customer** — Anyone who receives the output of a process (external or internal).

**Kano model** — Must-be, one-dimensional, attractive, indifferent and reverse requirements.

**Margin of error** — Half-width of a confidence interval for a survey estimate.

**Market segmentation** — Grouping customers with similar needs.

**Net promoter score (NPS)** — % promoters (9–10) − % detractors (0–6) on likelihood to recommend.

**Non-response bias** — Distortion when respondents differ from non-respondents.

**Operational definition** — Characteristic, method, sampling, decision criteria and source that make a measurement repeatable.

**Output requirement / service requirement** — About what is delivered / about how the customer is treated.

**Specification limit** — Boundary of acceptable performance for each unit or transaction.

**Stated / unstated needs** — Needs customers say / assume or haven't imagined.

**Target** — Ideal value of a CTQ.

**VOB, VOC, VOE, VOP** — Voices of the business, customer, employee and process.

**Y = f(X)** — Outputs are a function of process inputs.
