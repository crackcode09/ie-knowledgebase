# Module 4 — Where to Go for Customer Requirements

**Time:** 30 min

## Learning objectives

1. List sources of customer requirements and what each is good for.
2. Combine sources into a requirements inventory.

## 1. Sources

(Figures in the examples column are illustrative.)

| Source | Good for | Spare-parts example |
|---|---|---|
| Contracts, quality agreements, POs | Formal, binding requirements | Distributor agreement: 98% line-fill; ASN 24 h before delivery |
| Standards and regulations | Must-comply requirements | Export control screening; packaging regulations |
| Complaints, returns, warranty | Failures, frequency, cost | Top complaint themes: wrong part, no status, late |
| Service and chat logs, call recordings | Customer language; repeated questions | "Where is my order?" = 38% of calls |
| Web analytics and search logs | What customers try to do | Most frequent searches are competitor part numbers |
| Sales and lost-order reasons | Why we lose | "Competitor ships today," "couldn't find the part" |
| Competitor and other-industry benchmarks | What's becoming normal | Same-day shipping by cut-off time in consumer e-commerce |
| Interviews, site visits | Unstated needs, context | Technicians photograph worn parts with phones |
| Surveys | Importance, satisfaction, prevalence | Module 5 |
| Front-line employees | Process failure points | Order desk re-keys emailed orders |

## 2. Requirements inventory

Put everything in one list with source, segment and type before analysis:

| Requirement (customer words) | Source | Segment | Output / service | Stated / unstated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "I need it before the next shift" | Interviews | MRO | Output | Stated |
| "Don't make me call to find out where it is" | Calls, survey | All | Service | Stated |
| Line-fill ≥ 98% | Contract | Distributor | Output | Stated (formal) |
| Find the part from a photo of the old one | Site visits | MRO | Service | Unstated (not yet imagined) |
| Part arrives undamaged | — (assumed) | All | Output | Unstated (must-be) |

## Check yourself

Which sources best reveal unstated needs?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Observation/site visits and in-depth interviews (plus Kano surveys to confirm); complaint data and surveys mostly reflect stated needs.
</details>
