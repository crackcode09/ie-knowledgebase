# Module 1 — Obtaining the Voice of the Customer; Listening to All the Voices

**Time:** 45 min

## Learning objectives

1. Organize and manage a VOC research effort.
2. Choose collection methods for the question at hand.
3. Balance VOC with the voices of the business, employees and process.

## 1. A VOC plan

| Step | Spare-parts case |
|---|---|
| 1. Purpose and decisions it will inform | Redesign ordering and delivery to grow spare-parts sales |
| 2. Customers and segments to hear from | MRO plants, distributors, OEMs (Module 2) |
| 3. What we already know (reactive data) | 14 months of complaints, lost-order reasons, web analytics |
| 4. Qualitative research | 24 interviews (8 per segment), 3 site visits to plant stores |
| 5. Quantitative research | Survey of 300 customers: importance, satisfaction, Kano, acceptable delivery time |
| 6. Analysis and translation | Affinity → CTQ tree → specifications → C&E matrix |
| 7. Owners, budget, timeline | Marketing (survey), CI engineer (analysis), 8 weeks |

## 2. Methods

| Reactive (already flowing in) | Proactive (you go and get it) |
|---|---|
| Complaints, returns, warranty claims | Interviews |
| Service calls, chat logs | Site visits / observation |
| Sales notes, lost-order reasons | Focus groups |
| Web analytics, search terms | Surveys |
| Online reviews | Customer advisory panels |

**Interviews and observation** discover needs (especially unstated ones). **Surveys** measure how widespread and important known needs are. Use both, in that order.

**Interview tips:** open questions; ask about the last time something went wrong ("Tell me about the last time a bearing failed and you needed a replacement"); follow with "why?"; record verbatims.

## 3. Listening to all the voices

| Voice | Whose | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| **VOC** — customer | Buyers, users, influencers | Needs and priorities |
| **VOB** — business | Leadership, finance | Margin, growth, strategy, constraints |
| **VOE** — employee | Customer service, warehouse, sales | Where the process fails customers; practical ideas |
| **VOP** — process | Data from the process itself | What the process can actually deliver today (capability, lead time) |

A good design balances them: same-day shipping (VOC) must be affordable (VOB), workable for the warehouse (VOE), and achievable by the process (VOP).

## Check yourself

Why is complaint data alone a poor basis for a redesign?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

It comes only from customers who complain, captures problems but not priorities or delighters, and misses customers who quietly left.
</details>
