# Module 16 — Capstone: Design a Process with DCDOV

**Time:** 4–6 h

## The brief

Your company is adding a **kitting cell** that packs repair kits (8–12 small parts, a bag, a label and an instruction card) for service technicians. Demand: 240 kits per 7.5-hour shift. Customers are field technicians and the service planners who order the kits.

Work through DCDOV and produce a short design report (one page or slide per item).

| # | Deliverable | Module | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Charter: opportunity, scope, goals, team | 0 | |
| 2 | VOC: list of at least 8 needs from technicians and planners; affinity groups; Kano type for each | 1 | Interview two people if you can |
| 3 | CTQs with operational definitions; House of Quality with 4–6 technical requirements | 2 | E.g., kit accuracy, kitting time, label legibility |
| 4 | Three concepts (e.g., manual bench with pick-to-light, flow rack with scale check, semi-automated) and a Pugh matrix | 3 | Iterate once |
| 5 | Predicted capability of the most important CTQ | 4 | E.g., count accuracy as DPMO |
| 6 | PFMEA of the selected concept, top 5 failure modes, actions | 6 | Severity first |
| 7 | Error-proofing for the top 3 failure modes | 7 | Wrong part, missing part, wrong label |
| 8 | Design scorecard with RTY | 8 | |
| 9 | Takt, task list with precedence, line balance | 13 | 7.5 h × 60 / 240 = 1.875 min takt |
| 10 | Simulation of the cell's flow (modify `simulate_line`) | 11–12 | Include a variable step |
| 11 | Control plan and standard work for one station | 14 | |
| 12 | Verification plan: pilot success criteria and sample size | 14–15 | |

## Rubric (per deliverable)

| 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Missing or generic | Present, partly justified | Specific, quantified, traceable to customer needs |

20+ of 24: ready to lead a DFSS project. Traceability is the key test — every design choice should trace back to a customer need through the QFD.
