# Further Reading

Editions are the commonly cited ones; check for newer editions.

| Source | Why read it |
|---|---|
| Creveling, C. M., Slutsky, J. L., & Antis, D. *Design for Six Sigma in Technology and Product Development*. Prentice Hall, 2003. | Source of the CDOV/DCDOV-style roadmap for product development |
| Yang, K., & El-Haik, B. *Design for Six Sigma: A Roadmap for Product Development*, 2nd ed. McGraw-Hill, 2009. | Comprehensive DFSS reference |
| Pugh, S. *Total Design*. Addison-Wesley, 1991. | Concept selection method (Module 3) |
| Akao, Y. (ed.). *Quality Function Deployment*. Productivity Press, 1990. | QFD from its originator (Module 2) |
| Kano, N., et al. (1984). "Attractive quality and must-be quality." *Journal of the Japanese Society for Quality Control*, 14(2). | Kano model (Module 1) |
| AIAG & VDA. *FMEA Handbook*, 1st ed. 2019. | Current automotive FMEA method with Action Priority (Module 6) |
| Shingo, S. *Zero Quality Control: Source Inspection and the Poka-Yoke System*. Productivity Press, 1986. | Error-proofing (Module 7) |
| Boothroyd, G., Dewhurst, P., & Knight, W. *Product Design for Manufacture and Assembly*, 3rd ed. CRC Press, 2010. | DFM/DFA (Module 7) |
| Montgomery, D. C. *Design and Analysis of Experiments*. Wiley. | DOE (Module 9) |
| Hopp, W. J., & Spearman, M. L. *Factory Physics*, 3rd ed. Waveland, 2011. | Little's law, Kingman/VUT, variability and flow (Modules 11–12) |
| Little, J. D. C. (1961). "A proof for the queuing formula L = λW." *Operations Research*, 9(3), 383–387. | Little's law |
| Rother, M., & Harris, R. *Creating Continuous Flow*. Lean Enterprise Institute, 2001. | Takt, line balancing, cell design (Module 13) |
| Abernethy, R. B. *The New Weibull Handbook*, 5th ed. 2006. | Weibull analysis (Module 15) |
| NIST/SEMATECH *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*, Ch. 5 (DOE) and Ch. 8 (Reliability). https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ | Free reference for Modules 9 and 15 |
