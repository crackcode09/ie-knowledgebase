# Glossary

**Affinity diagram** — Groups raw comments into themes that express underlying needs.

**B10 life** — Time/distance by which 10% of units have failed.

**Benchmarking** — Studying competitors' or best-in-class solutions to learn why they work.

**Control plan** — Document listing each controlled characteristic, method, frequency and reaction plan.

**CTQ** — Critical-to-quality: a measurable characteristic with target and limits, traced to a customer need.

**DCDOV / DMADV** — Design roadmaps: Define, Concept, Design, Optimize, Verify / Define, Measure, Analyze, Design, Verify.

**DFM / DFA** — Design for manufacture / for assembly.

**DOE** — Design of experiments: planned changes to several factors at once to model their effects.

**Error-proofing (poka-yoke)** — Design that prevents mistakes or makes them immediately obvious.

**FMEA** — Failure mode and effects analysis; DFMEA (design) and PFMEA (process).

**Kano model** — Classifies needs as must-be, one-dimensional (performance), attractive (delighter) or indifferent.

**Lifecycle cost** — Total cost from development through manufacture, use, service and disposal.

**Little's law** — WIP = throughput × flow time.

**Monte Carlo simulation** — Repeated random sampling of inputs to obtain the distribution of an output.

**Pilot** — Small-scale real-conditions trial before full launch.

**Pugh matrix** — Concept comparison against a datum using +, 0, −.

**QFD / House of Quality** — Matrix linking customer needs to technical requirements.

**RSS** — Root sum of squares tolerance analysis.

**RTY** — Rolled throughput yield: probability a unit passes all steps defect-free the first time.

**Scorecard (design)** — Predicted performance of CTQs and process steps vs targets.

**Standardized work** — Takt, work sequence and standard WIP for a job.

**Success-run test** — Zero-failure test sized to demonstrate reliability at a confidence level.

**Takt time** — Available time divided by customer demand.

**Transfer function** — Y = f(X): a model predicting a CTQ from its inputs.

**Weibull distribution** — Flexible life distribution with shape β and scale η.
