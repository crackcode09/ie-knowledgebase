# Module 11 — A3 Problem Solving

**Time:** 45 min

## Learning objectives

1. Tell the story of a kaizen on one A3 sheet.
2. Use the A3 as a coaching and communication tool.

## 1. What an A3 is

A single sheet of A3 paper (≈ 11 × 17 in) that tells a problem-solving story from left to right, top to bottom. Used at Toyota for proposals, problem solving and status reports (Sobek & Smalley, *Understanding A3 Thinking*, 2008). The constraint of one page forces clear thinking.

## 2. Layout and the carriage cell A3

| Left side | Right side |
|---|---|
| **1. Background** — Carriage demand rose to 560/day; customers' lead time expectation 5 days | **5. Countermeasures** — U-cell, 4 operators; preload SMED (50 → 17 min); kanban kitting (9 cards); point-of-use parts; error-proofing |
| **2. Current condition** — Output 465/day vs 560; WIP 5,170; lead time 9.2 days; preload effective C/T 112 s vs takt 90 s; walking 100 m/unit | **6. Implementation plan** — Who / what / when table, incl. 30/60/90 actions |
| **3. Goal** — ≥ 560/day; WIP −50%; lead time ≤ 4 days; no added headcount | **7. Results / follow-up** — Pilot 87 s/unit; 15 days after: 545/day average (Module 12); WIP count at day 15 (illustrative) 1,950 → lead time 1,950 / 560 ≈ 3.5 days |
| **4. Root cause analysis** — Preload constraint: long trial-and-error changeovers, fixture loading inside the cycle, parts not at point of use; push scheduling builds inventory | **8. Next steps** — Close gap to 560/day: preload TPM (uptime 85% → 92%), second preset fixture |

A blank template is in `templates/a3-template.md`.

## 3. Using A3s well

- Write it **with** the team, in pencil — it changes as you learn.
- Use pictures, charts and numbers instead of paragraphs.
- The coach (sponsor or Lean leader) asks questions rather than giving answers: "How do you know?" "What's the root cause?" "How will you check?"

## Check yourself

Why is "Implement a U-cell" not a good entry for box 4 (root cause)?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

It's a countermeasure, not a cause. Box 4 should explain why the problem exists; countermeasures follow from it in box 5.
</details>
