# Module 10 — Job Analysis

## Objectives

- Describe the purpose and outputs of job analysis.
- Collect job information systematically.
- Connect job analysis to work measurement, training and job evaluation.

## What is job analysis?

**Job analysis** systematically identifies the tasks, responsibilities, knowledge, skills, abilities and working conditions of a job. Work measurement tells you **how long** tasks take; job analysis tells you **what** the job is and **what it takes** to do it.

Outputs:

- **Job description** — purpose, duties and tasks, responsibilities, working conditions.
- **Job specification** — knowledge, skills, abilities, education, experience, physical requirements.
- **Task inventory** — tasks with frequency, time share and importance (where work sampling and standards help).

## Methods of collecting information

| Method | Strength | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Observation (including work sampling) | sees the real job | misses infrequent or mental tasks |
| Interviews with job holders and supervisors | rich detail | memory and bias |
| Questionnaires / task checklists | many people, comparable data | wording, low response |
| Work logs / diaries | captures variation over time | effort for the job holder |
| Existing documents (SOPs, training, standards) | quick | may be out of date |
| Critical-incident review | what distinguishes good from poor performance | needs many incidents |

Use more than one method and validate the result with job holders and supervisors.

## Example: Marlow kitter (abbreviated)

| Task | Frequency | Share of time (work sampling) | Importance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pick parts to kit list | continuous | 47.7 % (picking) | high |
| Walk to locations | continuous | 16.5 % | (waste to reduce) |
| Complete paperwork / scan | per kit | 8.8 % | medium |
| Search for parts | frequent | 7.8 % | (waste to reduce) |
| Wait for system | frequent | 6.9 % | (waste to reduce) |

Specification extract: read part numbers and kit lists accurately; use handheld scanner; lift up to 15 kg occasionally; stand and walk for most of the shift.

## How job analysis connects

- **Work measurement** — task lists define what to measure; standards quantify each task.
- **Methods improvement** — task time shares reveal waste (walking, searching above).
- **Training and qualification** — the specification defines the "qualified worker" assumed by the standard.
- **Job evaluation and pay** — compares jobs on factors such as skill, effort, responsibility and conditions (see [Introduction to Industrial Engineering](../../introduction-to-industrial-engineering/README.md)).
- **Ergonomics and safety** — physical requirements and conditions.
