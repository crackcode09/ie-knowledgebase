# Further Reading

The course author listed these titles from general knowledge. Editions and details were **not verified** when this list was written, so check the current editions before buying.

- Freivalds, A., and Niebel, B. W. *Niebels' Methods, Standards, and Work Design*. McGraw-Hill. The standard text for every topic in this course.
- Kanawaty, G. (ed.). *Introduction to Work Study*. International Labour Office (ILO). Includes allowance tables and practical procedures.
- Meyers, F. E., and Stewart, J. R. *Motion and Time Study for Lean Manufacturing*. Prentice Hall.
- Zandin, K. B. (ed.). *Maynard's Industrial Engineering Handbook*. McGraw-Hill. Has chapters on time study, PMTS and standard data.
- Barnes, R. M. *Motion and Time Study: Design and Measurement of Work*. Wiley. A classic.
- The MTM and MOST systems are taught through their licensing bodies and training providers. Check them for current system details.
