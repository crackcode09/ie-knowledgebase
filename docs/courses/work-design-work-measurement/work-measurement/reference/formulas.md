# Formula Sheet

## Time study

- Element time (continuous timing) = reading at the end of the element − reading at the end of the previous element
- Observed time = mean of the valid element times
- Normal time = observed time × rating / 100 (machine-controlled elements use a rating of 100)
- Occasional element per unit = element normal time ÷ frequency (units per occurrence)
- Standard time:
  - ST = NT × (1 + A), with allowance as a % of normal time
  - ST = NT ÷ (1 − A), with allowance as a % of the workday
- Allowance as a % of the workday converted to a % of normal time = A ÷ (1 − A)
- Units per hour = 60 ÷ standard minutes
- Standard hours per 100 units = standard minutes × 100 ÷ 60

## Number of cycles (±k accuracy)

- n = (z · s / (k · x̄))²
- z = 1.96 for 95% confidence
- For small pilot samples, use t with n − 1 degrees of freedom instead of z

## Leveling

- Rating factor = 1 + Σ(skill, effort, conditions, consistency adjustments)

## Labor reporting

- Earned hours = Σ units × standard hours per unit
- Efficiency = earned hours ÷ hours on standard
- Utilization = hours on standard ÷ paid hours
- Productivity = earned hours ÷ paid hours = efficiency × utilization

## Work sampling

- p̂ = observations in category ÷ total observations
- 95% confidence interval: p̂ ± 1.96 √(p̂(1 − p̂)/n)
- Sample size: n = z² p(1 − p) / e²
- p-chart limits for daily data: p̄ ± 3 √(p̄(1 − p̄)/nᵢ)
- Standard per unit = (total time × share of time on the task × rating ÷ units) × (1 + allowance)

## Learning curve

- Tₙ = T₁ · n^b
- b = log(learning rate) / log 2

## Staffing and takt

- Operators = demand × standard time ÷ (available minutes per operator × planned efficiency)
- Takt time = available time ÷ demand

## Predetermined motion time systems

- 1 TMU = 0.00001 hour = 0.036 seconds
