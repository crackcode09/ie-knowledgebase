# Further Reading

## Opened and checked when the course was written

- U.S. Office of Personnel Management, *Human Capital Framework* overview: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/human-capital-framework/tabs/overview/ (the four systems and the plan–implement–evaluate cycle; cites 5 CFR Part 250, Subpart B).
- National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine (2020), *Facilities Staffing Requirements for the Veterans Health Administration: Resource Planning and Methodology for the Future*, Chapter 4: https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25454/chapter/6 (baseline-plus-variance models, workload and infrastructure parameters, non-available time, KPIs, annual review).

- Kanawaty, G. (ed.). *Introduction to Work Study*. International Labour Office (ILO).
